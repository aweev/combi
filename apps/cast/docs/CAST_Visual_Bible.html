01 — Brand Foundation

What Cast is

"The social operating system where truth is currency and secrets are the product."

Design Philosophy

Cast's identity lives at the intersection of the sleek and the savage. Every surface is minimal, dark, and precise — but underneath runs a current of danger. The aesthetic declares: this is real. this matters.

The darkness is not decoration. It is the environment where secrets live. The red is not an accent — it is alarm, exposure, revelation.

Three Design Principles
01. Cinematic Precision
Every interaction feels like a scene. Votes are verdicts. Reveals are executions. The UI never feels casual — it feels consequential.
02. Dangerous Clarity
The blur is the product. What is hidden must be felt before it is revealed. Clarity is earned, not given. The UI enforces this at every paywall.
03. African Vernacular
The energy draws from West and Central African internet culture — bold, sharp, in on the joke. The UI speaks Lagos and Douala before it speaks anywhere else.

08 — Voice & Tone

How Cast speaks

Four emotional modes. Each screen knows which one it is. Copy never drifts between modes mid-screen.

01
Provocateur
Default · Feed · Voting · Leaderboards
"Your squad just Cast you.
Open it if you're brave."
Sharp. Direct. Has opinions. The voice that makes you feel like the app knows something you don't.
02
Intimate
Burn Book reveals · AI Persona · Unlock moments
"The truth is yours.
What you do with it is your business."
Quiet. Almost a whisper. Used when the user has just paid, just seen something they cannot unsee.
03
Hype
Wins · Streaks · Tournament victories · Badges
"Your squad WON the Lagos Cast War.
Accept their apologies."
Loud. Culturally fluent. Knows the slang. Used sparingly so it never loses impact.
04
Clinical
Transactions · KYC · Errors · Legal
"Transaction confirmed.
Reference: #8X7K-9N2M"
Flat. Precise. Zero personality. Cast drops the mask for legal and financial moments.
