# CONUS OPERATING BIBLE

## How CONUS works every day

**Version 1.0**

---

# 00 — THE OPERATING IDEA

CONUS is a system.

It is not one app feature.

It is not one magazine.

It is not one competition.

It is not one advertising product.

It is not one entertainment channel.

CONUS is an interconnected operating system for:

**Content
Creators
Audience
Participation
Competition
Community
Recognition
Rewards
Culture
Brands
Opportunities
Data
Events
IP**

The job of the operating system is to continuously move people and ideas through those layers.

The fundamental CONUS loop is:

> **DISCOVER → EXPERIENCE → PARTICIPATE → SIGNAL → RECOGNIZE → CREATE OPPORTUNITY → REPEAT**

---

### 1. DISCOVER

# 04 — THE FIVE FOUNDATIONAL SEED PROPERTIES

CONUS should launch with a deliberately small number of strong properties.

## 01 — DAILY CONUS

The daily habit.

### Format

**Topic → Story → Challenge → Quiz → Result → Ranking → Reward → Tomorrow teaser**

### Example

**Today's topic:**

> How well do you know Cameroonian music?

A short piece of content.

Five questions.

User receives:

> **8/10**

Then:

> **#12 in Kumba**

Then:

> **#47 in South-West**

Then:

> Share your result.

The next day:

> **Can you beat yesterday's score?**

---

# 02 — CONUS SHORT

The watching engine.

### Seed season

One original short-form series.

Example:

**THE LAST 500**

Six episodes.

3–6 minutes each.

Every episode creates another question.

> What would you do?

Audience discussion continues between episodes.

The same story creates:

* full episode
* short clips
* polls
* questions
* discussion
* quiz
* character profiles
* creator content
* sponsor integrations

# 03 — CONUS PITCH

The creator engine.

### Format

> **Have an idea? Pitch it.**

* idea
* category
* target audience
* format
* 60-second pitch
* sample

CONUS selects an initial group.

Produce pilots.

Release them.

Audience responds.

Strong concepts move forward.

# 04 — CONUS CHALLENGE

Audience votes.

Winner receives:

* prize
* recognition
* CONUS feature
* future opportunity

The competition itself produces content.

# 19 — SPONSOR INTEGRATION RULE

A sponsor should answer:

> **Why does this brand belong in this experience?**

Bad integration:

> Logo appears.

Better:

> Brand funds the challenge.

Better:

> Brand provides the tools participants need.

Better:

> Brand creates the opportunity.

Best:

> The audience understands why the brand made the experience possible.

# 41 — WHAT NOT TO BUILD FIRST

Do not initially prioritize:

* complex marketplace
* sophisticated recommendation engine
* native apps
* blockchain
* complicated AI systems
* huge creator economy
* dozens of categories
* national-scale operations before validation
* enormous reward pools
* hundreds of advertisers
* unnecessary microservices

First prove:

> **People return.**

Then:

> **People participate.**
