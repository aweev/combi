# CONUS ZERO BLUEPRINT

## The first 90 days of the CONUS system

**Version 1.0**

---

# 00 — WHAT CONUS ZERO IS

CONUS Zero is not the launch of the final CONUS.

It is the **controlled proof that the CONUS system works.**

The objective is not:

> Get as many users as possible.

The objective is to prove:

> **People will return, watch, participate, compete, share, follow people, recognize winners, and create demand for more.**

At the same time, CONUS must prove:

> **Creators want to participate.**

> **Businesses see commercial value.**

> **Sponsors are willing to fund experiences.**

> **The team can operate the system repeatedly.**

CONUS Zero is therefore an experiment in **audience behavior, entertainment, community, creator discovery, and commercial value.**

---

# 01 — THE CONUS ZERO THESIS

The first 90 days attempt to prove six things.

### 1. HABIT

People return because something new happens regularly.

### 2. ENTERTAINMENT

People watch because the content is genuinely interesting.

### 3. PARTICIPATION

People don't just watch; they vote, play, compete, share, and respond.

### 4. DISCOVERY

Unknown people and ideas can emerge through the audience.

### 5. COMMUNITY

Locations and communities rally around participants.

### 6. COMMERCIAL VALUE

Brands can fund experiences because CONUS creates meaningful attention.

If these six things work together, CONUS has a foundation worth scaling.

# 37 — SUCCESS GATES

CONUS Zero should not scale simply because 90 days have passed.

Scale only when there is evidence of:

### GATE 1

People return.

### GATE 2

People participate.

### GATE 3

People share.

### GATE 4

Creators want in.

### GATE 5

At least some concepts demonstrate repeatability.

### GATE 6

Businesses recognize commercial value.

### GATE 7

The team can operate the system reliably.

---

# 38 — FAILURE GATES

Pause and rethink if:

* people watch but never participate
* rewards are the only reason people participate
* competitions become follower contests
* content is consistently ignored
* creators don't see value
* sponsors only want conventional advertising
* local rankings create no behavior
* operational workload becomes unsustainable
* voting cannot be trusted
* the product is confusing

Failure is information.

Do not scale a broken loop.

---

# 40 — THE ZERO RULE

Never spend heavily to prove something that can be tested cheaply.

Examples:

Don't build a massive competition engine before proving people want competitions.

Don't build a marketplace before advertisers compete for inventory.

Don't build sophisticated recommendations before you have meaningful behavior data.

Don't create ten shows before one show proves audience demand.

Don't create a national leaderboard before a local leaderboard proves geographic competition matters.
