# CONUS BUSINESS & ECONOMIC MODEL

## 01. Purpose

The CONUS Business & Economic Model defines how the CONUS system creates economic value, who receives that value, how money moves through the system, and how the business becomes increasingly valuable as participation, content, creators, communities, and commercial demand grow.

CONUS is not fundamentally a subscription business.

It is not fundamentally an advertising business.

It is not fundamentally a media business.

It is an **attention, participation, entertainment, opportunity, and commercial-value system**.

The economic model exists to turn those behaviors into a sustainable business while preserving the integrity of the audience experience.

The central economic principle is:

> **Brands fund experiences. Audiences generate demand. Creators create value. CONUS organizes the system.**
