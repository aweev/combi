# Architecture Decision Records (ADR) — combi

Records for decisions taken in the `combi/` monorepo. Format follows the
precedent set in `api/docs/adr/0000.adr.md`: **Status · Context · Decision ·
Consequences · Alternatives Considered**.

## Why 0001–0010 exist

The Circle product specification set (`apps/circle/*.md`, 21 documents,
~16,700 lines) contained no authority hierarchy and no cross-references, so
contradictions between documents were unresolvable. Each ADR below arbitrates
one such contradiction and names the surviving source text. `apps/circle/`
documents then point at these ADRs instead of restating them.

## Status Legend

- **Proposed** — recommendation drafted, awaiting founder sign-off. Documents
  may already be written against it, but the decision is not final.
- **Accepted** — decision made; contradicting document text has been rewritten.
- **Deprecated** — no longer recommended but still referenced somewhere.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a newer ADR (link it).

## Money gate

ADR-0003, 0004, 0005 and 0006 change price, billing behaviour or brand. They
stay **Proposed** until explicitly approved; treat their Decision sections as
recommendations, not as settled law. The remaining six are structural and
arbitrate wording conflicts without moving money.
